How it works

From spreadsheet to buzzers in three steps

1

Create your quiz

Add each question to a spreadsheet with its category and point value, Jeopardy-style. Add pictures if you like.

Download the sample quiz →

2

Start a game

Upload your spreadsheet and check the board. Share the room code or join link, then hit Start once everyone's in.

3

Buzz in and play

Pick a tile to put the question on the shared screen. Players buzz in on their phones, you see who buzzed first, and you score each answer on the board.

Questions

Good to know

Is it really free?

Yes. No sign-up, no ads and nothing to install.

Do players need an account?

No. They open the join link or type the room code, then enter a name.

Are our quiz and scores saved?

No. A room only lives in the server's memory while you play, and it's gone once the server restarts. Your quiz stays safe in your own spreadsheet.

What if someone's phone drops off?

Reopening the page puts them straight back in the game, in the same place in the buzz queue.

How do I create a quiz?

Start from the sample quiz: a zip with a spreadsheet and an optional folder of pictures. The control center explains the format when you upload, and points out any row that needs fixing.

Can questions have audio or video?

Not yet. Questions and answers can show pictures, but not sound or video clips.

Can I suggest a feature or report a bug?

Yes, please! Open an issue on GitHub to suggest a feature or report a bug.
