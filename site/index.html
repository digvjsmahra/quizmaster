How it works

From spreadsheet to buzzers in three steps

1

Write the quiz

Fill in a spreadsheet: one row per question, each with a category and a points value. Add pictures if you like.

Download the sample quiz to start from.

2

Open a room

Host a quiz, upload your spreadsheet and check the board. Then share the 4-letter room code or the join link.

3

Buzz in

Pick a tile and the question goes up on the shared screen. Players race to buzz, you see who was first and by how many milliseconds, and you score each answer.

Questions

Good to know

Is it really free?

Yes. No sign-up, no ads and nothing to install.

Do players need an account?

No. They open the join link or type the room code, then enter a name.

What happens to our quiz and scores?

Nothing is stored. A room only lives in the server's memory while you play, and it's gone once the server restarts. Your quiz stays safe in your own spreadsheet.

What if someone's phone drops off?

Reopening the page puts them straight back in the game, in the same place in the buzz queue.

How do I write a quiz?

Start from the sample quiz: a zip with a spreadsheet and an optional folder of pictures. The control center explains the format when you upload, and points out any row that needs fixing.
